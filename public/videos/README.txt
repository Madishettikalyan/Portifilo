Place your motion graphic videos, Instagram reels, and long commercial videos here:

Supported formats:
- .mp4 (Recommended for web compatibility)
- .webm
- .mov

Examples:
- reel-1.mp4 (Vertical 9:16 video for Shorts/Reels)
- film-1.mp4 (Widescreen 16:9 video for Brand Commercials)

You can also use YouTube or Vimeo links directly in `data/portfolioData.ts`:
Example:
videoUrl: '/videos/reel-1.mp4'  OR  'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...'
